# Decision-model recommendation: retrospective evidence annotation

Date: 2026-10-11. Public-source proposal; not operational guidance.
Reviewed [README](https://github.com/Ethical-Tech-CoLab/mariupol-evacuation-model/blob/47af9d5decd9502a1084cc5958c0ee5b0e175e05/README.md).
This document-led assessment does not validate the severity/feasibility model.

## Recommendation

Test a bounded public-source support checker for historical claims, with exact
dates, location, provenance and quoted passages. Return supports / contradicts /
not established / insufficient evidence. Keep severity calculations,
feasibility evidence and modeled nighttime-light behavior outside the model.

The README explicitly limits what can be inferred about the delay between
modeled severity and negotiated evacuation. A decision model cannot infer
what decision-makers knew, prove why negotiations were delayed or establish
legal responsibility from that gap.

## Test and alternatives

Compare a human chronology/source checklist with a local classifier, Jev's
[citation recipe](https://docs.typesafe.ai/cookbooks/citation_check.md),
Microsoft-Decision-1 and Sage on approved public material.
Split by event/source/date blocks. Include retrospective knowledge presented as
contemporaneous, modeled versus observed light loss, and uncertain reporting.

Measure unsupported inference acceptance, relevant-evidence recall, calibration
and reviewer time. Treat adjacent dates sharing one source as correlated.
Account for retrieval, calls and human correction in total review cost.

## Gate

No new evacuation recommendation, phase threshold, legal conclusion or
operational map. Preserve the retrospective label and existing caveats.
Only source-linked, reversible annotations may be added after human review.
Exact proprietary checkpoints are not available locally; no savings measured.
